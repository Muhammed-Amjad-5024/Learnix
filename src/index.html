What Learnix provides

Everything you need in one academic space.

Learnix is designed to make academic resources, communication and classroom access easier to manage.

📚

Organized Resources

Access notes, PDFs, presentations, documents, important links and educational videos in a structured environment.

🏫

Classroom Based

Students access academic resources through authorized classrooms and subjects.

💬

Private Communication

Students and faculty can communicate privately for academic questions and resource requests.

🔔

Notifications

Stay informed about new resources, classroom requests, messages and important updates.

🔎

Search & Discover

Find academic resources without searching through hundreds of unrelated messages.

🔐

Permission Based

Access is controlled according to institutions, roles, classrooms and authorized relationships.
